# 🛒 Swiggy Instamart Growth Analysis

> A business-focused analytics solution designed to identify revenue drivers, category opportunities, city-tier performance, shop-type performance, and sales growth patterns to support data-driven business growth decisions.

---

# 📌 Executive Summary

Business growth depends on understanding where revenue is being generated, which markets are performing strongly, which categories drive sales, and where performance is declining.

This project analyzes Swiggy Instamart business data using Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to identify key business performance patterns and potential growth opportunities.

The analysis focuses on transforming business data into actionable insights that can help decision-makers understand revenue concentration, market performance, shop-type contribution, customer ratings, and sales trends.

---

# 🚨 Business Problem

Businesses operating across multiple categories, markets, and store formats need clear visibility into performance to identify growth opportunities and detect areas of declining performance.

Without structured business analysis, it can be difficult to:

- Identify major revenue-driving categories
- Compare city-tier performance
- Understand shop-type contribution
- Detect declining sales trends
- Identify high-potential markets
- Evaluate customer experience
- Prioritize business growth opportunities

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing business performance across multiple dimensions.

# 🚀 Business Growth Opportunities

The analysis identifies several areas that could support future business growth.

### 🥦 1. Strengthen High-Performing Categories

Fruits and Vegetables contributes approximately **14.82%** of total sales.

Snack Foods contributes approximately **14.60%**.

Together, these categories contribute approximately **29.42% of total sales**.

**Growth Opportunity:**

- Protect inventory availability
- Reduce stockout risk
- Optimize category assortment
- Analyze demand patterns
- Evaluate promotional opportunities

---

### 🌍 2. Explore Tier 3 Market Potential

| City Tier | Sales |
|---|---:|
| Tier 1 | ~336K |
| Tier 2 | ~393K |
| Tier 3 | ~472K |

Tier 3 generates the highest sales among the analyzed city tiers.

**Growth Opportunity:**

Investigate the factors behind Tier 3 performance, including:

- Customer demand
- Product preferences
- Category mix
- Pricing
- Product availability
- Local market characteristics

Successful strategies can then be evaluated for application in other markets.

---

### 🏪 3. Learn From High-Performing Shop Types

Supermarket Type1 generates approximately **787.67K** in sales.

This makes it the strongest sales contributor among the analyzed shop types.

**Growth Opportunity:**

Investigate:

- Category mix
- Store characteristics
- Location
- Product availability
- Customer demand
- Operational performance

The objective is to understand which characteristics are associated with stronger performance.

---

### 📉 4. Address Sales Decline

Sales declined from approximately **308K in 2021 to 290K in 2024**.

### Overall Change: **-5.84%**

This represents an important business signal.

**Growth Opportunity:**

Further investigation should focus on:

- Store location
- Customer acquisition
- Category mix
- Product availability
- Store maturity
- Pricing
- Promotions
- Operational performance

The analysis identifies the performance gap, while deeper analysis is required to determine the root cause.

# ⚠️ Business Impact Note

This project does **not** claim that the analysis directly increased revenue because the recommendations were not implemented in a live business environment.

Instead, the project demonstrates the ability to identify:

- Revenue drivers
- Market opportunities
- Performance gaps
- Growth opportunities
- Business risks
- Areas requiring further investigation

The **-5.84% sales change**, **29.42% contribution from the top two categories**, **~472K Tier 3 sales**, and **~787.67K Supermarket Type1 sales** are analytical findings from the dataset.

These findings should be treated as **decision-support insights**, not measured real-world business outcomes.

---

# 📌 Project Limitations

- The dataset may not represent complete real-world Swiggy Instamart operations.
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish causation.
- Business recommendations have not been tested in a live environment.
- Actual financial impact cannot be measured without implementation data.
- Further store-level and customer-level analysis would be required for deeper strategic decisions.
