# Swiggy Instamart Business Growth Analysis
## Project Documentation

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Swiggy Instamart business performance to identify key revenue drivers, growth opportunities, category performance, city-tier opportunities, shop-type performance, and changes in sales over time.

The analysis focuses on using business data to understand **where growth is coming from, where performance is declining, and which areas may offer opportunities for improvement**.

Power BI, Power Query, and DAX were used to transform raw business data into structured analysis and actionable business insights.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to evaluate business performance and identify opportunities that can support sustainable growth.

The analysis focuses on:

- Identifying major revenue drivers.
- Finding high-performing categories.
- Identifying high-potential city tiers.
- Evaluating shop-type performance.
- Measuring sales growth and decline.
- Identifying areas of underperformance.
- Understanding customer rating performance.
- Converting analytical findings into business growth opportunities.

## 5. Revenue Driver Analysis

Category performance was analyzed to determine which products contribute most strongly to overall sales.

### Key Finding

Fruits and Vegetables contributes approximately **14.82%** of total sales.

Snack Foods contributes approximately **14.60%**.

Together, these categories contribute approximately:

**29.42% of total sales.**

### Growth Opportunity

These categories represent major revenue drivers.

The business can focus on:

- Maintaining product availability.
- Reducing stockout risk.
- Optimizing product assortment.
- Understanding customer demand.
- Evaluating promotional opportunities.

### Business Impact

Protecting high-performing categories can help maintain the existing revenue base while creating opportunities for further category-level growth.

## 8. Sales Growth Analysis

Sales performance was analyzed across opening years.

| Opening Year | Sales | YoY Change |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2021 | 308K | — |
| 2022 | 305K | -0.97% |
| 2023 | 299K | -1.97% |
| 2024 | 290K | -3.01% |

### Overall Change

Sales declined from approximately **308K in 2021 to 290K in 2024**.

**Overall change: -5.84%**

### Key Finding

The analysis identifies a downward sales trend across the analyzed opening-year cohorts.

### Business Growth Problem

The important business question is not simply:

> "Sales declined by 5.84%."

The important question is:

> **"What is causing newer shop cohorts to generate lower sales?"**

### Growth Investigation Areas

Further analysis should examine:

- Store location
- Customer acquisition
- Category mix
- Product availability
- Store maturity
- Pricing
- Promotions
- Operational performance

### Business Impact

This finding identifies a clear area requiring management attention.

If the underlying drivers of the decline can be identified and addressed, the business may have an opportunity to improve the performance of newer shops.

## 10. Customer Experience & Growth

The overall average rating is approximately **3.92**.

Customer rating was analyzed alongside sales performance to avoid evaluating growth using revenue alone.

### Business Perspective

Sustainable growth should consider both:

**Revenue Performance + Customer Experience**

A business should not focus only on increasing sales if customer experience is deteriorating.

### Growth Opportunity

Future analysis can examine customer ratings by:

- Shop type
- City tier
- Category
- Store size

This could reveal areas where customer experience improvement may support long-term business growth.

## 12. Business Growth Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Protect Revenue Drivers

Prioritize high-performing categories through:

- Better availability

- Assortment optimization
- Targeted promotions

---

---

Analyze Supermarket Type1 to understand which operational and commercial characteristics contribute to stronger sales.

---

Focus on location, demand, category mix, availability, pricing, and customer acquisition.

Reduce excessive dependence on a limited number of categories by identifying underperforming categories with realistic growth potential.

## 16. Analytical Limitations

The findings are based on the available dataset and should be interpreted within its limitations.

- The dataset may not represent complete real-world Swiggy Instamart operations.
- The analysis identifies relationships and patterns but does not prove causation.
- Business recommendations have not been tested in a live environment.
- Actual financial impact cannot be measured without implementation data.
- Further store-level, customer-level, and time-series analysis would be required for stronger strategic conclusions.
